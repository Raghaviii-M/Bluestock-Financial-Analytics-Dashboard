# Bluestock-Financial-Analytics-Dashboard
An interactive Power BI report built during my Bluestock remote
internship to explore mutual fund industry trends, fund performance,
investor activity, SIP inflows, and Net Asset Value (NAV).
## Project Overview
This dashboard brings related mutual fund metrics into one report,
organised across five pages. It is designed to make key trends easier to
explore through visualisations, KPI cards, and report-level analysis.
## Dashboard Pages
1. Industry Overview --- Industry AUM trends from 2022 to 2025,
   with headline metrics including Total AUM, SIP Inflows, Total
   Folios, and Total Schemes.
2. Fund Performance --- Fund return and risk comparisons, along
   with NAV trends.
3. Investor Analytics --- Investor transaction patterns, including
   geographic and demographic breakdowns.
4. SIP & Market Trends --- SIP inflows, Nifty 50 trends, and
   category-level inflow analysis.
5. NAV Detail --- Detailed Net Asset Value trend analysis.
## Tools & Skills

- Financial and mutual fund analytics

## Key Insights
- AUM trends across 2022–2025.
- Overview of AUM, SIP inflows, folios and scheme count.

Developed a five-page interactive Power BI dashboard that consolidates mutual fund analytics, enabling streamlined exploration of industry trends, fund performance, investor activity, SIP inflows, and NAV movements.
